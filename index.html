Serviced

Know what’s due. Know what it costs.

Log a fill-up in three numbers and a service in one tap. Serviced tells you what’s due next and what the car is really costing you.

What it does

  • Fill-ups. Odometer, fuel, what you paid. Price per litre, consumption and cost per kilometre are worked out for you; partial fills are handled properly.
  • Services. Oil change, tyre rotation, tyre change, brakes, battery, air filter, inspection, registration, insurance, or anything else with its own name.
  • Reminders that follow distance and time. “Every 10,000 km or 6 months”, whichever comes first, with a notification two weeks before and on the day. Logging the service resets it.
  • What the car costs. This month’s spend, fuel and service apart; a history by month; CSV export.
  • All your cars. Each petrol or diesel car with its own log, reminders and currency.
  • Any make, any country. No VIN. Kilometres and litres or miles and gallons; any currency.

What it doesn’t do

There is no account, no sign-in, no syncing and no network code at all. Your garage is one file on your iPhone, and the only way anything leaves is the CSV export you choose to share.
