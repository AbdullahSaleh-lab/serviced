Privacy Policy — Serviced

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Serviced is built so that your car log stays with you. This policy explains how.

What we collect

Nothing. Serviced has no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics and no advertising, and it contains no third-party code. The app has no networking code at all: it never sends anything to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

Your cars (the names and details you type, the currency), your fill-ups (date, odometer, fuel, cost, note), your services (kind, date, odometer, cost, where, note), your odometer readings, your reminder settings and your choice of units. These are kept in the app’s own storage on your iPhone and are included in your device backup like any other app’s data, if you use backups. Deleting an entry removes it, deleting a car removes its whole log, and deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Sharing

Serviced shares a car’s log only when you tap Export CSV and pick where the file goes. What happens to it after that is up to the app or person you send it to.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked the first time you save a reminder or log a service, to remind you before something is due. The reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent from a server.

Serviced does not ask for your location, camera, photos or contacts.

Apple-provided data

The App Store gives developers aggregated, anonymous statistics such as download counts, depending on your device’s analytics-sharing setting. These never include anything you entered in the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
